Ad hoc

Align on these three today

1. Metric backbone

Brandwatch Europe decides which metrics sit in the market template. We do not add or drop KPIs market by market.

2. Decision dates

30 Sep locks the Q3 structure. 7 Oct is final client feedback on CS regional and Europe Q3 templates. 19 Oct is final feedback on the rest of the package.

3. Consistency rule

Once the quarterly is approved, the BMW / MINI templates package follows it. We adapt depth and focus; we do not invent a new design per cadence.

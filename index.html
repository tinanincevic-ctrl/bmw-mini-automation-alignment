Reporting IQ — automates all report types, via templates

Reporting IQ is the platform that will support report automation for every type: Q3 market, Q3 CS regional, Q3 Europe, plus the monthly, influencer, weekly and ad hoc templates. Q3 CS regional and Q3 Europe templates go into the platform first (7–13 Oct) so the 15 Oct client reports can run from there. The remaining four templates are implemented together (19–26 Oct). From 26 Oct, all templates live in Reporting IQ. That is not a second client-facing report delivery.
